<Section id="circuit" title="Circuit performance" kicker="04">

The genetic circuit was designed for a measurable output above a low basal level. Characterisation includes time-course expression, dynamic range across marker concentrations, and comparison of reporter options.

<Section id="device" title="Device & strip prototypes" kicker="05">

Paper-based strip prototypes were built to test whether biological output translates into a visible band a non-specialist could read. Control lines were included on every strip.

<Placeholder label="Strip prototype photos and readout comparison — data pending" />

Early bench tests focused on flow uniformity and control-line visibility before running biological samples.

</Section>

<Section id="comparison" title="Comparison to reference methods" kicker="06">

The ultimate question is whether AURA's signal moves earlier or more accessibly than methods farms already use. We frame comparison against:

- Somatic cell count (industry standard early indicator)
- Electrical conductivity (common inline proxy)
- Culture or PCR (confirmatory reference)

<Placeholder label="Side-by-side comparison table — pending paired samples" />

We do not claim clinical validation. Any comparison is exploratory and sample-limited.

</Section>
