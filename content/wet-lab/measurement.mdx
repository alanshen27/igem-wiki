<Section id="overview" title="Measurement overview" kicker="01">

Characterisation turns constructs into comparable data: how bright, how leaky, how fast, and over what concentration range. This page collects calibration methods and results as they land.

</Section>

<Section id="fluorescence" title="Fluorescence readout" kicker="02">

**Setup.** Plate reader or fluorimeter with excitation/emission matched to reporter; blank with media or lysate alone.

**Metrics.**
- Basal fluorescence (leakiness)
- Fold induction at saturating inducer / marker
- EC₅₀ from dose–response fit where applicable

<Section id="calibration" title="Calibration standards" kicker="04">

Spiked marker stocks at known concentration anchor biological readouts. Standards are prepared fresh or thawed from documented aliquots with chain-of-custody in the notebook.

</Section>

<Section id="units" title="Units & reporting" kicker="05">

We report in SI-friendly units with clear definition: RFU for fluorescence, absorbance for colour, and qualitative band intensity for strips until quantitative strip densitometry is validated.
