<Section id="milk" title="Milk sample preparation" kicker="02">

**Purpose.** Prepare consistent milk matrix for spiking and assay development.

**Outline.**
1. Collect or thaw milk aliquots; record source and date
2. Centrifuge to remove fat if required for assay format
3. Filter or dilute per assay SOP; document dilution factor
4. Spike marker at defined concentrations; include unspiked control

<Callout variant="lab" title="Matrix matters">
  Buffer-only controls are necessary but not sufficient — always include unspiked and spiked milk.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section id="binding" title="Binding screen" kicker="03">

**Purpose.** Compare recognition-element candidates against purified marker and milk matrix.

**Outline.**
1. Immobilise candidate on surface or capture format
2. Incubate with marker in buffer, then in diluted milk
3. Wash; apply detection reagent or coupled reporter
4. Read signal; compare to irrelevant-protein control

<Section id="expression" title="Cell-free / contained expression" kicker="04">

**Purpose.** Produce readable reporter output from assembled circuit.

**Outline.**
1. Prepare lysate or induce contained cells per biosafety approval
2. Add template DNA at defined concentration
3. Incubate at controlled temperature; time-course if needed
4. Quench and read fluorescence or colour

</Section>

<Section id="strip" title="Strip assembly & readout" kicker="05">

**Purpose.** Translate biological output to a lateral-flow style readout.

**Outline.**
1. Apply sample to well; allow capillary flow
2. Wait for control line to appear (required for valid test)
3. Read test line against reference card
4. Record photograph and time-to-result
