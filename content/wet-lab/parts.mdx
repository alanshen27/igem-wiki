<Section id="overview" title="Parts overview" kicker="01">

AURA parts are designed for biosensor screening and low-leak reporter expression. Registry IDs will be added on submission; sequences and intended use are documented here first.

<Callout variant="note" title="Characterisation">
  Parts without measurement data are labelled <em>uncharacterised</em>. Do not assume performance from sequence alone.
</Callout>

</Section>
