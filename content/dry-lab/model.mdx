<Section id="overview" title="Modeling overview" kicker="01">

Dry-lab models guide where to spend bench time: which promoter strength, which incubation window, and what detection threshold would matter on a farm. Models are theoretical until calibrated with wet-lab data.

</Section>

<Section id="kinetics" title="Binding & induction kinetics" kicker="02">

We model marker binding and downstream reporter induction as coupled processes. Parameters come from literature where available and from our own assays when measured.

<Section id="sensitivity" title="Sensitivity analysis" kicker="03">

Sensitivity analysis asks which parameters most affect time-to-signal and limit of detection: promoter leakiness, degradation rate, marker concentration, and milk matrix effects.

<ScienceExplainer
  accent="signal"
  analogy="Turning knobs on a mixing desk to see which one actually changes the sound — sensitivity analysis finds the knobs that matter."
  precise="Partial derivatives or Monte Carlo sweeps over parameter ranges identify which biochemical constants dominate output variance."
/>

</Section>

<Section id="thresholds" title="Decision thresholds" kicker="04">

Thresholds are framed with veterinary input: a screen should flag risk for follow-up, not diagnose. Model outputs suggest feasible regions; stakeholders set what is actionable.

</Section>

<Section id="limits" title="Limits" kicker="05">

<Callout variant="warn" title="Not a substitute for data">
  Models without calibration are hypotheses. We do not use unvalidated model output as evidence of clinical performance.
</Callout>

</Section>
